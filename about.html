Where it
stands.

Ravionics is a Umanah Systems Group aircraft program building persistent, unarmed aerial surveillance for governments, coast and port authorities and infrastructure operators.

The hard part of watching a coast is not seeing. It is staying. Ravionics builds for the long watch: an aircraft that needs no runway, rides its wing for hours, and a relay that hands over before anyone has to turn for home.

Where we are

Assessed awardable

Tradewinds Solutions Marketplace and DARPA ERIS assessed the Ravionics proposals as awardable.

In simulation

The design, the flight software and the ground control all run in simulation today. No flight has been made yet.

Every figure on this site is a design goal or an estimate.
