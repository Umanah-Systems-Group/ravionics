UTC --:--:-- / FREGATA

Named for the bird
that barely lands.

The frigatebird stays aloft over open sea for days on a long, efficient wing. FREGATA is built on the same idea: lift off without a runway, then ride a long wing for hours while ten small propellers rest.

Ready to lift

Wing on edge, ten propellers pointing at the sky, three legs on the pad. No runway, no catapult, no net.

One aircraft.
Six views.

  1. 01

    Front

  2. 02

  4. 04

All the way round

In cruise, from every side. The wingtip propellers turn; the other eight lie folded flat against their pods.
