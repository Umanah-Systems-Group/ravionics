Always Watching

FREGATA lifts off a ship's deck like a helicopter, turns its whole wing to fly like an airplane, and is built to stay on watch for a full day.

Scroll ↓

A coastline, a harbor or a pipeline needs watching every hour of every day. Crewed patrols are expensive and small drones come home after an hour. FREGATA needs no runway, rides a long wing for hours on two propellers, and hands over to the next aircraft before it leaves. It carries cameras, never weapons.

Deck to wing
in one smooth turn

01 · Lift

Straight up, off the deck

Wing on edge, all ten propellers pointing at the sky. The legs fold into the body as it climbs.

02 · Tilt

The whole wing turns

Wing and tail tilt forward together as speed builds. The body stays level the whole way, and the flight software holds it steady through every degree.

03 · Fold

Eight propellers rest

Once the wing carries the weight, eight propellers stop and fold flat against their pods.

04 · Cruise

Two are enough

The wingtip propellers pull FREGATA along on a few kilowatts. Hours of this is what a day on watch looks like.
