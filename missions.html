Search and recovery

Relieved before it has to turn home.

It flies the plan.

Takeoff, transition, the route, the orbit and the landing are flown by the aircraft. The crew watches, approves and steps in only when they choose to.

Point, and it goes.

Tap a new orbit, a new heading or a new height. The aircraft works out how to get there safely and tells you before it does.

Hands on the sticks.

A pilot can always take direct control. If the link drops, the aircraft takes back over, holds safe and heads for its planned recovery.
