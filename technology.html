Six systems.
One aircraft.

  1. 01

    Power

    Two small heavy-fuel diesels turn generators. They fly the aircraft in cruise and top up the battery.

  2. 02

    Battery

    Covers takeoff, landing and the transition, the few minutes when power demand peaks.

  3. 03

    Fuel

    One tank in the middle of the body, on the same heavy fuel the ship already carries.

  4. 04

    Flight computer

    Autopilot, safety checks and the radio link, with a backup that takes over on its own.

  5. 05

    Sensor ball

    A swappable pod under the nose with day and thermal cameras, looking down and out to sea.

  6. 06

    Wiring spine

    Power and data along one spine to all ten motors, with blind-mate plugs at every module joint.

Three ways to fly

Lift

Wing on edge, all ten propellers pointing at the sky. The battery helps the diesels through the few hungry minutes of vertical flight.

Off a ship's deck or a pad the size of a parking space.

Transition

Wing and tail tilt forward together as speed builds, until the wing carries the weight.

The hardest moment for a tilt-wing, and the one we simulate most.

Loiter

Eight propellers fold flat against their pods. The two at the wingtips pull FREGATA along on a few kilowatts, and the diesels recharge the battery.

Hours of this is a day on watch.

Motor out

Ten propellers share the load. If one stops, the others make up the difference and the aircraft heads for its recovery point.

Checked in simulation for every motor.
